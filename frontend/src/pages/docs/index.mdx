<h1 class="docs-h1"><span>NOX</span> // HOW TO PLAY</h1>
<p class="docs-lead">
Same keyboard. Two players. First to <strong>5 round wins</strong> takes the crown. No accounts needed - just sit down and play.
</p>

<div class="docs-grid">
  <div class="docs-card docs-card--cyan">
    <div class="docs-kicker"><i>01</i> THE IDEA</div>
    <h3 class="docs-h3">RUN • DODGE • SHOOT • SURVIVE</h3>
    <p class="docs-p">You both start on opposite sides of a small dark arena. Move around, dash through danger, shoot, grab power-ups, and avoid the floor trying to hurt you. Each round lasts up to a minute. Knock out the other player to win the round.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="docs-card docs-card--lime">
    <div class="docs-kicker"><i>02</i> START A GAME</div>
    <h3 class="docs-h3">PRESS PLAY → COUNTDOWN → FIGHT</h3>
    <p class="docs-p">Hit <strong>START DUEL</strong>. You get a 3-2-1 countdown, then go. If someone needs to leave, hit <strong>EXIT</strong> on their side - the other player wins.</p>
  </div>
</div>

<hr class="docs-hr" />

<div class="docs-card docs-card--cyan" style="margin-top:0">
  <div class="docs-kicker"><i>03</i> CONTROLS - ONE KEYBOARD</div>
  <table class="docs-table">

  <p class="docs-p" style="margin-top:10px">On touch screens: tap the left side to shoot as P1, right side as P2.</p>
</div>

<div class="docs-grid">
  <div class="docs-card docs-card--lime">
    <div class="docs-kicker"><i>04</i> MOVING</div>
    <h3 class="docs-h3">DASH TO STAY ALIVE</h3>
    <p class="docs-p"><strong>Dash</strong> makes you flash forward really fast and you cannot get hit while flashing. Use it to dodge bullets or slip through a tight gap. You need to wait a moment before you can dash again.</p>
    <p class="docs-p" style="margin-top:8px">You both move at the same base speed. There is a speed slider at the start screen if you want the whole game slower or faster.</p>
    <div style="margin-top:12px"><DocDashDemo client:visible /></div>
  </div>
  <div class="docs-card docs-card--amber">
    <div class="docs-kicker"><i>05</i> SHOOTING</div>
    <h3 class="docs-h3">FOUR KINDS OF BULLETS</h3>
    <ul class="docs-list">
      <li><strong>Normal</strong> - what you start with. Balanced and reliable. You never run out.</li>
      <li><strong>Needle</strong> - tiny and very fast. Does almost nothing from the front, but hits super hard if you shoot someone in the back. Great for sneaking around.</li>
      <li><strong>Cannon</strong> - big, slow and heavy. Hurts a lot when it lands, but you only get a few shots and it is easy to see coming.</li>
      <li><strong>Trick</strong> - bounces off walls. First bounce hits hardest, then it gets weaker each time it bounces.</li>
    </ul>
    <p class="docs-p" style="margin-top:8px">Special bullets come from pickups. You get a few shots, then you go back to normal.</p>
    <div style="margin-top:12px"><DocBulletDemo client:visible /></div>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="docs-card docs-card--pink">

<div class="docs-card docs-card--amber">
  <div class="docs-kicker"><i>07</i> WATCH OUT</div>
  <h3 class="docs-h3">WALLS • LAVA • SLIME • THE VOID</h3>
  <table class="docs-table">
    <thead><tr><th>DANGER</th><th>WHAT HAPPENS</th></tr></thead>
    <tbody>
      <tr><td><strong>Walls</strong></td><td>Dark blocks that block you and block most bullets. Trick bullets bounce off them.</td></tr>
      <tr><td><strong>Lava Vent</strong></td><td>An orange circle on the floor. It glows and blinks first to warn you, then turns bright red and burns you if you stand on it. Moves to a new spot every few seconds.</td></tr>
      <tr><td><strong>Slime</strong></td><td>A green patch that makes you move and dash slower while you are inside. It does not hurt you.</td></tr>
      <tr><td><strong>The Void</strong></td><td>After about 45 seconds the edge of the arena starts closing in. Green blocks crumble around you. If you stand outside you slowly lose health. Stay near the center.</td></tr>
    </tbody>
  </table>

<div class="docs-grid">
  <div class="docs-card docs-card--lime">
    <div class="docs-kicker"><i>08</i> HOW TO WIN</div>
    <h3 class="docs-h3">FIRST TO 5 ROUNDS</h3>
    <ul class="docs-list">
      <li>If you knock out the other player, you win the round and the next round starts.</li>
      <li>If time runs out and no one is knocked out, the player with more health wins the round. If health is tied it is a draw.</li>
      <li>First player to win 5 rounds wins the whole match and sees the victory screen.</li>
      <li>Leave a match with <strong>EXIT</strong> and the other player wins instantly.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
  <div class="docs-card docs-card--cyan">
    <div class="docs-kicker"><i>09</i> SCREEN GUIDE</div>
    <h3 class="docs-h3">WHAT YOU SEE</h3>
    <ul class="docs-list">
      <li><strong>Top corners</strong> - player name, score and health bar. The bar shakes a little when you get hit and glows when you heal.</li>
      <li><strong>Little icons</strong> under health show if you have triple shot, shield or dash boost, and how long is left. One chip shows your current bullet type and how many shots you have left.</li>
      <li><strong>Middle</strong> - round number and time left. The timer turns color when the void is closing or time is almost up.</li>
      <li><strong>Center</strong> - the arena where you run and shoot.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</div>

<div class="docs-callout">
<strong style="color:var(--nox-lime)">QUICK TIPS //</strong> Hold shoot instead of tapping. Dash <em>through</em> bullets, not away from them. Needle is best from behind. Cannon is best when you trap someone in a corner. Trick is best when you bounce it around a wall. Control the middle before the void closes. Press <strong>R</strong> for a quick rematch on the game over screen.
</div>
